FAQ

Questions firms ask us

Will my clients know you exist?

Only if you want them to. By default we work fully under your firm's name.

Which software do you use?

QuickBooks Online and Xero, plus common add-ons like A2X and AppFolio.

How do you keep client data secure?

We use accountant-user access, never client passwords, keep 2FA on every account, and sign your NDA before starting.

What hours do you work?

Our team works U.S. business hours (Eastern Time), so you get same-day answers.

Can we start small?

Yes. Most firms start with one or two client files as a pilot.

What about tax season?

Tell us your expected volume early and we'll plan capacity before January.
