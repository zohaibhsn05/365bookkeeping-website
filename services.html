Monthly Bookkeeping

Who it's for
Businesses that want their books done right every month without thinking about it.
What's included
  • Transaction categorization in QuickBooks Online or Xero
  • Bank and credit card reconciliations, to the cent
  • Accounts payable and receivable tracking
  • Month-end close
What you get
A Profit & Loss statement and Balance Sheet every month, and books that are always ready for your CPA.

Cleanup & Catchup

Who it's for
Businesses whose books are messy, wrong, or months (or years) behind.
What's included
  • Review of your current file and a written list of issues
  • Fixing miscategorized transactions and duplicate entries
  • Rebuilding broken reconciliations and opening balances
  • Catching up every missing month
What you get
Accurate, current books and a short summary of what we fixed, so you can file taxes with confidence.

Payroll, AP & AR

Who it's for
Businesses that run payroll and manage bills and invoices.
What's included
  • Recording payroll, taxes, deductions, and payroll liabilities
  • Entering and tracking bills and vendor payments
  • Invoicing and tracking customer balances
What you get
Payroll and balances that tie out correctly, every month.

Property Management Bookkeeping

Who it's for
Property managers, landlords, and real estate businesses.
What's included
  • AppFolio and QuickBooks bookkeeping
  • Owner and tenant ledgers
  • Property-level income and expense reporting
What you get
Clear numbers for every property, and owner reports you can send with confidence.

E-commerce & Inventory

Who it's for
Shopify stores and Amazon sellers.
What's included
  • Sales, fees, refunds, and payouts recorded correctly (A2X, MyWorks)
  • Inventory reconciliation
  • Cost of goods sold (COGS) tracking
  • Product-level profitability
What you get
Your real profit after fees, returns, and inventory, not just your sales number.

Setup & Migration

Who it's for
New businesses, businesses switching software, or owners managing more than one company.
What's included
  • New QuickBooks Online or Xero setup
  • Chart of accounts design and cleanup
  • QuickBooks Desktop to Online migration
  • Multi-entity bookkeeping
What you get
A clean, organized file set up the right way from day one.
